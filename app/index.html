    About this guide

    Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published before launch. Each guide is written for this page, in its own words, and names the source of each fact — PowerPyx, PSNProfiles, Exophase, GTA Zone or GTABase. Where a trigger has not been confirmed by those sources, the guide says so instead of guessing. There are no online trophies: GTA VI has no online mode at launch, so the whole list is earnable solo.
