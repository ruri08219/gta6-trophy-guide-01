Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026. The trophy names, descriptions and grades here are the official list as PlayStation publishes it (Exophase and PSNProfiles agree on all 55). Every guide is written in our own words from those descriptions plus the launch-era guides cited beside each trophy. Where the cited guides have not yet confirmed an exact trigger, the guide says so instead of inventing one — check the cited source again as more players confirm it. Keep cheats off while you chase trophies: GTABase and TechWiser report that cheats disable trophies and saving.

One trophy may be missable

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy PowerPyx and GTABase flag as possibly missable: the first meditation, Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, disappears after Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story — PowerPyx reports no mission gold medals, no mission replay, and one playthrough is enough.
